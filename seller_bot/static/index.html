Sathi steps in at the three moments that decide delivery

On WhatsApp and calls, in Hindi, Hinglish, English and regional languages. Buyers don't install anything and can always still pay cash.

Right after the order

Confirm and pin the address

Checks the buyer still wants it, gets a landmark and location pin, and offers to pay by UPI.

Out for delivery

Make sure someone's home

If not, it books a better day or fixes the address before the rider sets out.

Minutes after a failed attempt

Recover the delivery

Asks what really happened, books a reattempt, and records the buyer's word if the rider never came.
